Three Hardware Tiers for Every Vehicle

From low-cost commuter bikes to high-performance touring motorcycles and custom automobiles.

Tier 1: Microcontroller Lite

AEZEL Lite (ESP32-S3)

Instant-boot 60 FPS LVGL cluster with BLE Turn-by-Turn HUD mirroring real Google & Apple Maps.

  • ✓ 240 MHz Dual-Core Xtensa ESP32-S3
  • ✓ 3.5"–4.3" SPI / 8080 Parallel Display (480×320)
  • ✓ BLE Turn-by-Turn HUD (Official Maps via navHUD)
  • ✓ Dual Sensors (Hall/RPM/Float) + Automotive CAN
  • ✓ Cold Boot: < 300 ms (Instant-On)
Est. BOM Cost $20 – $35
Tier 2: RISC-V Automotive

AEZEL Auto (ESP32-P4)

Direct MCU-based Wireless Android Auto projection with VCU CAN telemetry overlay.

  • ✓ 400 MHz Dual RISC-V ESP32-P4 SoC
  • ✓ 4.3"–7.0" MIPI-DSI Touch Display (800×480)
  • ✓ Wireless Android Auto Projection (via esp32p4-aa)
  • ✓ Hardware PPA 2D Accelerator + esp_h264
  • ✓ Cold Boot: < 1.2 seconds
Est. BOM Cost $45 – $65
Tier 3: Infotainment Pro

AEZEL Pro (Raspberry Pi / Linux)

Full Apple CarPlay & Android Auto head unit with zero-copy GStreamer video and VCU gauges.

  • ✓ Raspberry Pi 4/5 / CM4 / CM5 (Quad ARM)
  • ✓ 5.0"–10.25" Ultrawide Touch Display (1080p)
  • ✓ Full Apple CarPlay & Android Auto (via LIVI)
  • ✓ Siri / Google Assistant & 2-Way Audio
  • ✓ Split-Screen: 70% Navigation + 30% AEZEL VCU
Est. BOM Cost $85 – $130

Engineered for Open-Source Mobility

Every subsystem built for reliability, real-world harness safety, and seamless mobile connectivity.

🏍️

6 Built-in Vehicle Presets

Pre-configured profiles for Bikes (5/6-speed manual, lean angle), Scooters (CVT automatic, belt wear), and Cars (OBD-II, PRND, dual G-force).

🧭

Real Google & Apple Maps

Zero in-app map clones. Runs with your official navigation apps via BLE notification listener (Android) and native ANCS / MapKit (iOS).

🛰️

Dual-Source GNSS Telemetry

Filtered u-blox NEO-6M hardware GPS ($GPRMC/$GPGGA) with seamless automatic fallback to smartphone fused GPS if satellite lock is lost.

📱

Flutter Companion App

Single cross-platform codebase for iOS & Android providing live cockpit gauges, vehicle preset switching, remote controls, and trip replay.

🛡️

Automotive-Grade Protection

Opto-isolated discrete inputs, load-dump TVS clamping, RC low-pass ignition coil filter, and battery voltage supervision.

📶

One-Tap Wireless OTA

Update cluster firmware over Wi-Fi softAP (AEZEL-VCU-AP) straight from your phone or browser without removing the motorcycle dash.
